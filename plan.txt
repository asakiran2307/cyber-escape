Instead of complex cipher math or low-level console scripting, this beginner-to-medium edition relies on observation, logical common sense, and visual pattern recognition. Any student with a smartphone can participate and have fun without feeling stuck or intimidated.

2. Station Puzzles & Ready-to-Use Questions 
Station 1: The Phishing Detector (Social Engineering) 
Where to place: Front door or North Wall (A4 Poster: Station 1) 
What opens when scanned: A screen showing 3 email screenshots or text blocks: 
• Email A: 'URGENT: You won a 5,000 gift voucher! Click claim-gift-fast.biz right now!' (Red flag: Suspicious domain & urgency). 
• Email B: 'Campus Library: Your book issue receipt #4481 is attached. View portal at library.college.edu.' (Real email: Authentic official college domain). 
• Email C: 'IT Support: Your password expires in 10 minutes. Send your current password here to keep account.' (Red flag: IT never asks for your password). 
Question to Players: 'Which email is genuine and safe to open? (A, B, or C)' 
Answer: Email B. 
Screen Output: When they select Email B, the page says: 'Correct! Never click unknown links. Your Passcode is: SAFE-PASS-101 (Go to Station 2)'. 
Station 2: The Social Media Password Trap (OSINT / Human Factor) 
Where to place: East Corner / Table (A4 Poster: Station 2) 
Gatekeeper: Enter passcode 'SAFE-PASS-101' to view the clues. 
What opens when scanned: A simulated Instagram profile of a campus administrator named 'Rahul': 
• Bio: 'Proud dog dad to my golden retriever Sunny! Joined college in 2024.' 
• Photo Post: Picture of a golden retriever with caption: 'Sunny is 2 years old today!' 
Question to Players: 'Rahul used a common weak password pattern: Pet Name + College Joining Year. What is his password?' 
Answer: Sunny2024 (or sunny2024). 
Screen Output: Entering Sunny2024 reveals: 'Account unlocked! Weak passwords based on pets are easy to guess. Your Passcode is: LOCK-OUT-404 (Go to Station 3)'. 
Station 3: Fake Link Inspection (Lookalike URLs) 
Where to place: South Corner / Desk (A4 Poster: Station 3) 
Gatekeeper: Enter passcode 'LOCK-OUT-404' to unlock. 
What opens when scanned: A screen showing 4 links trying to imitate the college exam portal: 
1. www.portal-college-exam.free-host.ru
2. www.exam-college.org.fake-login.com
3. www.college.edu/exam-portal
4. www.col1ege-exam.com (look closely at the '1' instead of 'l') 
Question to Players: 'Which link is the REAL official portal?' 
Answer: Link 3 (www.college.edu/exam-portal). 
Screen Output: Selecting Link 3 reveals: 'Spot on! Real institutions use their official .edu or official registered domain. Your Passcode is: SHIELD-77 (Go to Station 4)'. 
Station 4: Master Emergency Disarm (Quick Recap) 
Where to place: Central screen or Game Master table (A4 Poster: Station 4) 
Gatekeeper: Enter passcode 'SHIELD-77' to access the final disarm console. 
What opens when scanned: A 3-question quick quiz: 
1. What was the pet name used in Station 2? -> Sunny
2. Which domain suffix did the real college site use in Station 3? -> .edu
3. What should you do if an email asks for your password? -> Report/Delete it 
Final Resolution: Submitting all 3 correct answers flashes a green screen: 
Result: CONGRATULATIONS! SYSTEM DEFENDED SUCCESSFULLY!
ROOM EXIT PIN: 5599 
Teams call out '5599' to the Game Master to stop the clock! 
3. Super Easy Implementation (Google Forms) 
You can create this entire event in 15 minutes using Google Forms with no technical hosting needed: 
Step 1: Create a Google Form named 'Cyber Escape Room'. 
Step 2: Create 4 Sections in the Form (one section for each station). 
Step 3: In Section 2, 3, and 4, add a Short Answer question asking for 'Enter Previous Station Key'. Turn on Response Validation -> Regular expression -> Matches -> enter the key. 
Step 4: Generate 4 QR codes using any free QR generator (e.g., qr-code-generator.com or Canva) pasting your Form link. 
Step 5: Print the QR codes on 4 plain A4 sheets with labels: 'Station 1: Email Check', 'Station 2: Social Media', 'Station 3: Link Audit', 'Station 4: Final Disarm'.
